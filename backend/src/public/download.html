Installing

  1. Tap Download above.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. Android will warn about installing from your browser — choose Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Needs Android 7.0 or newer. If you already have an older build, uninstall it first.

Signing in

Enter your mobile number and you'll get a one-time code. New numbers are taken straight to a short sign-up form.
